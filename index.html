My Dearest Apology

To the one who holds my heart

My love,

I sit here with a heavy heart, realizing how much I have taken your beautiful presence for granted. You, the daughter of Rizwan Irshad, who brings light into every corner of my life, deserve nothing less than my complete devotion.

Every moment without your smile feels like an eternity. I am sorry for the times I failed to show you how deeply I cherish you. Your laughter is my favorite melody, your touch my safest home. Forgive me for any pain I caused, intentional or not. I promise to love you more fiercely, listen better, and make you feel like the queen you truly are.

You are my peace, my passion, my everything. Let me make it up to you every single day for the rest of our lives.

Yours eternally,
Your adoring husband

JAUN ELIA

"Tumhein jo yaad karte hain toh dil ko sukoon milta hai,

When I remember you, my heart finds peace,
Without you, life has no meaning.
FIRAQ GORAKHPURI

"Tumhari yaad aati hai, toh aise lagta hai,
Jaise har pal mein tum hi ho."

When your memory comes, it feels
as if you are in every moment.

You are the poetry I never knew I could live.
